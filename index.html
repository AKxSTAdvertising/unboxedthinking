FAQ

Questions? Answers.

What does Unboxed Thinking do?

We're a digital marketing and web studio. We build websites and e-commerce stores, run Google, Meta, LinkedIn, Pinterest and WhatsApp campaigns, manage social media, and improve how brands show up on Google and in AI answers through SEO, AEO and GEO.

What are AEO and GEO?

AEO (answer engine optimisation) helps your business become the direct answer in Google's AI Overviews, featured snippets and voice search. GEO (generative engine optimisation) helps AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity understand, trust and mention your brand.

Do you work with businesses outside India?

Yes. Alongside brands in India we've built websites for companies based in the UAE, and we work remotely with clients wherever they are.

Can you handle both the website and the marketing?

Yes. The website, the campaigns and the search visibility are planned together, so every channel sends people to a page built to convert them.

How do we start a project?

Message us on WhatsApp or email with what you need. We'll understand the business, suggest the right direction and share a clear plan and timeline before any work begins.
